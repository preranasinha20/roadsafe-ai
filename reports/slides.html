Explainable ML for Road Accident Severity & Black Spots

Predict severity. Locate black spots. Explain every decision.

UK STATS19 · 2.05 M accidents · 2005-2017
AUCML (TE7483) · AIML · Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Pune

1

Problem and motivation

  • Injury accidents: 85% Slight, 14% Serious, 1.3% Fatal
  • Authorities need where to act and why, with a limited budget
  • Gap: accuracy-only reporting, random splits, no link between prediction, hotspots and explanation
  • RQ1 drivers of severity · RQ2 black-spot drivers · RQ3 value of leak-free spatial history
2

Data and leakage control

  • 2,046,815 clean rows (0.02% dropped, every step logged)
  • Casualty count and police attendance excluded: consequences of severity
  • Features: scene, time, location and expanding-window spatial history built from earlier years only
3

Pipeline

Clean → Engineer → Leak-free history → Time split (train 2006-14 · val 2015 · test 2016-17) → Pipeline (impute + one-hot) → Imbalance strategy → Model → Calibrate + decision scales (on 2015) → Test once → SHAP / LIME / black spots → Web app

Everything fitted on training years only; resampling never touches validation or test.

4

Models and imbalance study

  • Majority, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, LightGBM, XGBoost
  • None · class weights · under-sampling · SMOTE · tuned decision scales
  • Optuna tuning on validation macro-F1; isotonic calibration; 5-fold CV + paired t-tests
  • Finding: after scale tuning all strategies land within ~0.01 macro-F1, so the cheap one wins

6

Ablations and honesty

  • Removing spatial history, time features or coordinates each lowers macro-F1 and AUC
  • Scene-only features cap performance: vehicle and casualty tables would be the next gain
  • Distribution shift in 2016-17 (Serious+Fatal 15.5% → 18.6%) handled by calibration on 2015
  • Leakage columns shown in the ablation to quantify what they would add
7

Black-spot detection

  • 150 m severity-weighted (EPDO 10/5/1) peak detection; DBSCAN/HDBSCAN rejected as primary (chaining)
  • 6,000 sites from 2005-14; 95% of top-100 persist in 2015-17
  • Control sites persist 85%, so we also report rank correlation; sensitivity over radius and weights
8

Explainability

  • SHAP: global, per-class, dependence, local waterfalls, inside vs outside black spots
  • LIME compared with SHAP, with seed-stability measured
  • Speed limit, local history and hour dominate; weather is minor
9

Application, limits and future work

  • Static React app on GitHub Pages: map predictor, in-browser XGBoost + calibration + exact TreeSHAP, black-spot map, model lab (parity-tested vs Python)
  • Limits: UK-only, no traffic exposure, injury-only, scene-level features
  • Future: join vehicle/casualty tables, Indian MoRTH data, exposure-normalised risk, spatio-temporal forecasting
